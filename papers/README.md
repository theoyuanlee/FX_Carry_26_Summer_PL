# `papers/` — the reference literature held locally

Two papers, both cited in the report. The project's full bibliography is §Appendix of
[`../cesare/FX_Carry_Strategy_Project_Plan.md`](../cesare/FX_Carry_Strategy_Project_Plan.md); these
are the two held as PDFs in the repo.

| Paper | Cited for |
|---|---|
| **Lustig, Roussanov & Verdelhan (2011)**, *Common Risk Factors in Currency Markets* | The DOL and HML-FX factor construction. `final/report/05_return_drivers_and_crash_risk.md` regresses each track's daily returns on these factors |
| **Burnside, Eichenbaum & Rebelo (2011)**, *Carry Trade and Momentum in Currency Markets* | The carry/momentum interaction behind the Stage-5 momentum overlay — tested and rejected |

**Not held locally, but cited:** Menkhoff, Sarno, Schmeling & Schrimpf (2012a, 2012b) on global FX
volatility and currency momentum; Du, Tepper & Verdelhan (2018) on CIP deviations, which is the
literature the D3 cross-currency-basis work sits in; Farhi, Fraiberger, Gabaix, Rancière & Verdelhan
on crash risk in currency markets.
